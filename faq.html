What is GEO?

What is GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)?

GEO is the practice of optimizing content so it gets cited, referenced, or surfaced by AI-powered search engines like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude, Grok, and Bing Copilot. Unlike SEO which optimizes for click-through rates on Google search results pages, GEO optimizes for inclusion in AI-generated answers.

How is GEO different from SEO?

SEO optimizes for Google's ranking algorithm to drive clicks to your website. GEO optimizes your content for AI engines that generate answers directly — the goal is getting cited as a source. Key differences: GEO targets training data patterns and retrieval systems, uses structured data more aggressively, and optimizes for citation frequency rather than click-through rate.

Do I need GEO if I already have good SEO?

Yes. SEO and GEO serve different purposes. Strong SEO does not guarantee strong GEO performance. Many websites with excellent Google rankings are invisible on ChatGPT and Perplexity. As AI-powered search grows, GEO is becoming an essential complement to traditional SEO.
